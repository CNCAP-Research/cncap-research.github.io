About CNCAP

Science, education and conservation

The Centre for Nature Conservation and Applied Peace (CNCAP) is an independent research and educational initiative based in Australia. It aims to make geospatial science accessible and support the sustainable management of natural environments through applied research, open educational resources and reproducible scientific methods.

Research

Study environmental change and ecosystem dynamics using Earth observation and spatial analysis.

Education

Develop practical tutorials, open code and learning materials for students, researchers and professionals.

Applied peace

Encourage environmental cooperation and sustainable relationships between people and nature.

Research themes

Areas of interest

CNCAP brings together environmental science and geospatial methods to examine changes in natural and human-modified landscapes.

01 / CONSERVATION

Nature conservation

Ecosystem dynamics, habitat monitoring, biodiversity and environmental change.

02 / OBSERVATION

Earth observation

Optical and radar satellite imagery, time-series analysis, and environmental indicators.

03 / SPATIAL SCIENCE

GIS and spatial modelling

Spatial statistics, mapping, geospatial data integration and decision support.

04 / COMPUTATION

GeoAI

Machine learning, deep learning, image classification and geospatial automation.

05 / COASTS

Coastal and wetland environments

Mangroves, wetlands, shoreline dynamics and coastal adaptation.

06 / LAND MOTION

InSAR and land deformation

Satellite radar interferometry for monitoring land motion and subsidence.

Research directions

Potential areas for future CNCAP projects and open scientific workflows.
