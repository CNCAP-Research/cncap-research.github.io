Research theme 07 · ENVIRONMENT

Climate change and natural hazards

Climate variability, environmental hazards and their implications for ecosystems and communities.

All research themes

Research scope

Specific research topics

07.01

Agricultural drought

Assess drought intensity, duration and spatial variability using climate and satellite-derived indicators.

07.02

Soil salinity

Investigate spatial and temporal salinity variation using field measurements, spectral indicators and environmental models.

07.03

Flood and inundation

Map inundation and assess flood exposure using terrain, hydrological information and Earth observation.

07.04

Hydroclimatic variability

Examine changes in rainfall, temperature, soil moisture and evaporative demand in relation to ecosystem responses.

07.05

Multi-hazard assessment

Explore the spatial interaction of multiple hazards using explicit assumptions and uncertainty assessment.

07.06

Climate change adaptation

Investigate adaptation options under changing environmental conditions and alternative climate scenarios.

The topics describe research interests and potential investigations. Existing publications by the founder are listed separately with their original authorship and affiliations.
