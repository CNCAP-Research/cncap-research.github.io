Research · CNCAP

Research themes

CNCAP brings together environmental science, Earth observation and geospatial methods to investigate ecosystem dynamics, environmental change, sustainable resource management and emerging connections between the environment, society and peace.

Nine thematic areas and 54 research topics define the scope of CNCAP's research interests and proposed directions.

Areas of interest

01 / CONSERVATION

Nature conservation

Ecosystem dynamics, biodiversity, habitat condition and ecological restoration.

  • Ecosystem monitoring and assessment
  • Biodiversity and habitat conservation
  • Ecological restoration
  • Ecosystem services

02 / OBSERVATION

Earth observation

Satellite and airborne observations of environmental processes and landscape change.

  • Optical and radar remote sensing
  • Spectral indices and indicators
  • Satellite time-series analysis
  • UAV remote sensing
Explore research →
03 / SPATIAL SCIENCE

GIS and spatial modelling

Geospatial methods for understanding spatial patterns, processes and environmental relationships.

  • Spatial statistics and interpolation
  • Spatiotemporal analysis
  • Spatial regression and GWR/MGWR
  • Spatial decision support
Explore research →
04 / COMPUTATION

GeoAI

Artificial intelligence and computational methods for geospatial mapping, prediction and interpretation.

  • Machine learning
  • Deep learning
  • Semantic segmentation
  • Object detection and extraction

05 / COASTS

Coastal and wetland environments

Coastal ecosystem dynamics, shoreline change and adaptation to environmental pressures.

  • Mangrove, saltmarsh and seagrass ecosystems
  • Wetland dynamics
  • Shoreline erosion and accretion
  • Coastal blue carbon
Explore research →
06 / LAND MOTION

InSAR and land deformation

Satellite radar interferometry for examining surface deformation, subsidence and uplift.

  • Differential SAR interferometry
  • Persistent Scatterer InSAR
  • Small Baseline Subset InSAR
  • Vertical land motion
Explore research →
07 / ENVIRONMENT

Climate change and natural hazards

Climate variability, environmental hazards and their implications for ecosystems and communities.

  • Agricultural drought
  • Soil salinity

  • Hydroclimatic variability
Explore research →
08 / SUSTAINABILITY

Sustainable development and resource management

Spatial evidence for sustainable management of land, water and environmental resources.

  • Sustainable land use
  • Water resource management
  • Sustainable agriculture
  • Blue and green economies
Explore research →
09 / PEACE

Environment, society and applied peace

Emerging research direction

An emerging research direction on environmental pressures, community resilience and peaceful resource governance.

  • Environmental security
  • Natural resource conflicts
  • Community resilience
  • Environmental justice
