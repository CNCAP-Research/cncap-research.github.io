Research theme 02 · OBSERVATION

Earth observation

Satellite and airborne observations of environmental processes and landscape change.

All research themes

Research scope

Specific research topics

02.01

Optical and radar remote sensing

Investigate complementary spectral and microwave observations of vegetation, water and land surfaces.

02.02

Spectral indices and indicators

Evaluate index sensitivity, limitations and calibration for environmental monitoring.

02.03

Satellite time-series analysis

Analyse temporal variability, trends, seasonality and abrupt changes using consistent observation records.

02.04

UAV remote sensing

Explore fine-resolution aerial imagery for local habitat assessment, mapping and field validation.

02.05

Land use and land cover change

Quantify landscape transitions and examine their spatial and temporal patterns.

02.06

Multi-sensor data integration

Combine observations across sensors while addressing differences in resolution, geometry and acquisition timing.

The topics describe research interests and potential investigations. Existing publications by the founder are listed separately with their original authorship and affiliations.
