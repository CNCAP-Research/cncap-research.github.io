Research theme 03 · SPATIAL SCIENCE

GIS and spatial modelling

Geospatial methods for understanding spatial patterns, processes and environmental relationships.

All research themes

Research scope

Specific research topics

03.01

Spatial statistics and interpolation

Characterise spatial dependence and estimate environmental variables at unsampled locations with uncertainty assessment.

03.02

Spatiotemporal analysis

Investigate how spatial patterns evolve through time, including clustering and persistent or emerging changes.

03.03

Spatial regression and GWR/MGWR

Examine spatially varying associations and evaluate model assumptions, scales and predictive performance.

03.04

Spatial decision support

Integrate spatial evidence and explicit decision criteria to support transparent management choices.

03.05

Environmental suitability modelling

Assess relative suitability for ecological restoration, land use and other environmental applications.

03.06

Spatial data infrastructure

Investigate data organisation, metadata, interoperability and reproducible spatial information management.

The topics describe research interests and potential investigations. Existing publications by the founder are listed separately with their original authorship and affiliations.
