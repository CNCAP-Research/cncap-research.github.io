Research theme 09 · PEACE

Environment, society and applied peace

An emerging research direction on environmental pressures, community resilience and peaceful resource governance.

Emerging research direction

All research themes

Research scope

Specific research topics

09.01

Environmental security

Explore how environmental pressures, resource access and livelihood conditions relate to human security.

09.02

Natural resource conflicts

Investigate the spatial dimensions of competing resource uses, access and governance without assuming environmental change directly causes conflict.

09.03

Community resilience

Explore community capacities and vulnerabilities in responding to environmental change and resource pressures.

09.04

Environmental justice

Examine the distribution of environmental risks, benefits and access to resources across communities.

09.05

Participatory GIS

Explore ethical, consent-based approaches to integrating local knowledge and community priorities into spatial analysis.

09.06

Environmental peacebuilding

Investigate opportunities for cooperative environmental management and shared-resource governance as potential contributions to peaceful coexistence.

This theme is a proposed future direction. Its topics describe areas for exploration and collaboration; they are not claims of existing CNCAP projects, expertise or publications.
