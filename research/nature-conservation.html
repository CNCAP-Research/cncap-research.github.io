Research theme 01 · CONSERVATION

Nature conservation

Ecosystem dynamics, biodiversity, habitat condition and ecological restoration.

All research themes

Research scope

Specific research topics

01.01

Ecosystem monitoring and assessment

Assess changes in ecosystem extent, structure and condition using field observations and spatial indicators.

01.02

Biodiversity and habitat conservation

Characterise habitat distribution, fragmentation and connectivity to inform biodiversity conservation.

01.03

Ecological restoration

Monitor restoration trajectories and evaluate changes against reference conditions and management objectives.

01.04

Ecosystem services

Investigate the distribution of ecosystem functions and benefits, including carbon storage and coastal protection.

01.05

Nature-based solutions

Examine the performance and limitations of ecosystem-based interventions under different environmental conditions.

01.06

Conservation planning

Integrate environmental evidence and spatial priorities to support conservation decisions.

The topics describe research interests and potential investigations. Existing publications by the founder are listed separately with their original authorship and affiliations.
