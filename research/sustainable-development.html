Research theme 08 · SUSTAINABILITY

Sustainable development and resource management

Spatial evidence for sustainable management of land, water and environmental resources.

All research themes

Research scope

Specific research topics

08.01

Sustainable land use

Investigate land-use transitions, competing demands and environmental consequences to inform land management.

08.02

Water resource management

Explore water availability, watershed processes and resource pressures using spatial and temporal information.

08.03

Sustainable agriculture

Examine agricultural land condition, climate exposure and resource management practices.

08.04

Blue and green economies

Explore spatial planning for economic activities consistent with ecosystem condition and environmental constraints.

08.05

Environmental planning

Integrate spatial evidence into land and resource planning, considering alternative scenarios and trade-offs.

08.06

Sustainable Development Goals

Explore geospatial indicators relevant to selected environmental and resource-related Sustainable Development Goals.

The topics describe research interests and potential investigations. Existing publications by the founder are listed separately with their original authorship and affiliations.
