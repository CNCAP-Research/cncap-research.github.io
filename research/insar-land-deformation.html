Research theme 06 · LAND MOTION

InSAR and land deformation

Satellite radar interferometry for examining surface deformation, subsidence and uplift.

All research themes

Research scope

Specific research topics

06.01

Differential SAR interferometry

Investigate surface displacement using interferometric phase differences and appropriate corrections.

06.02

Persistent Scatterer InSAR

Explore long-term deformation estimation from stable radar scatterers.

06.03

Small Baseline Subset InSAR

Examine displacement time series using networks of interferograms with limited baselines.

06.04

Vertical land motion

Investigate vertical motion using observation geometry, assumptions and independent measurements such as GNSS.

06.05

Land subsidence and uplift

Map deformation patterns and examine their relationship with geological, hydrological and human factors.

06.06

Spatiotemporal deformation analysis

Characterise trends, spatial heterogeneity and potential deformation hotspots while accounting for measurement uncertainty.

The topics describe research interests and potential investigations. Existing publications by the founder are listed separately with their original authorship and affiliations.
