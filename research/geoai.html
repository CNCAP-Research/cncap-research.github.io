Research theme 04 · COMPUTATION

GeoAI

Artificial intelligence and computational methods for geospatial mapping, prediction and interpretation.

All research themes

Research scope

Specific research topics

04.01

Machine learning

Develop and evaluate predictive and classification workflows using geospatial and environmental variables.

04.02

Deep learning

Investigate neural network architectures and their transferability across geographic regions and sensors.

04.03

Semantic segmentation

Evaluate pixel-level mapping of environmental features and built environments from imagery.

04.04

Object detection and extraction

Identify and delineate spatial objects using image-based algorithms and independent validation.

04.05

Explainable artificial intelligence

Examine predictor contributions and model behaviour while distinguishing predictive associations from causal effects.

04.06

Geospatial workflow automation

Develop reproducible computational workflows for processing, analysing and documenting spatial data.

The topics describe research interests and potential investigations. Existing publications by the founder are listed separately with their original authorship and affiliations.
