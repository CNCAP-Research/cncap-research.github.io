About CNCAP

Connecting science, education, industry, and society

The Centre for Nature Conservation and Applied Peace (CNCAP) is an independent, non-commercial, founder-led research and educational initiative established by Dr Thuong Tran in Melbourne, Australia, with research and educational interests in Australia, Viet Nam, and the wider international community.

Who we are

CNCAP integrates environmental science, Earth observation, geographic information systems (GIS), spatial modelling, and geospatial artificial intelligence (GeoAI) to investigate environmental change and develop practical approaches to real-world problems.

The initiative aims to bridge academic research, education and industry by translating scientific knowledge, and geospatial methods into practical applications for nature conservation, environmental management, infrastructure, natural resource management, and sustainable development.

CNCAP is currently managed and operated solely by its founder, Dr Thuong Tran. It welcomes opportunities for collaboration with academic institutions, industry partners, government agencies, non-governmental organisations, and individual researchers who share its research and educational interests.

Mission and vision

Our mission

To advance geospatial science through applied research, open education, and industry-oriented applications, translating scientific knowledge into practical solutions for environmental management, nature conservation, sustainable development, and informed decision-making.

Our vision

To develop CNCAP as an independent platform connecting academia, industry, and society through scientific research, geospatial innovation, knowledge exchange, and environmental cooperation, contributing to healthy ecosystems, sustainable livelihoods, and peaceful coexistence.

Objectives

What we aim to do

Applied environmental research

Investigate environmental change, ecosystem dynamics, and natural hazards using Earth observation and spatial analysis.

Conservation and sustainable resource management

Apply spatial evidence to ecosystem monitoring, restoration, land and water management, and coastal adaptation.

Open education and professional learning

Develop practical GIS, remote sensing and GeoAI tutorials, teaching datasets, and learning resources for students and practitioners.

Industry-oriented applications

Translate geospatial research and analytical methods into workflows relevant to industry and government decision-making.

Reproducible science

Promote transparent methods, documented workflows, appropriate data sharing, and accessible research code.

Environmental cooperation and peace

Explore relationships between environmental pressures, community resilience, natural resource governance, and peaceful coexistence.

Environmental peacebuilding is an emerging research interest.

Academia and industry

Industry engagement and practical applications

CNCAP seeks to connect scientific research with practical geospatial needs in environmental consulting, engineering, infrastructure, utilities, government and natural resource management. The following are areas of expertise and potential collaboration.

Environmental consulting and monitoring

Satellite imagery analysis, ecosystem condition assessment, land cover change, and environmental indicators for monitoring and reporting.

Infrastructure and asset management

Spatial data integration, feature extraction, mapping, and geospatial analysis for infrastructure planning, asset inventories and monitoring.

Spatial planning and natural resource management

Suitability analysis, spatial decision support and environmental assessments for land, water and coastal resource management.

Geospatial data and GeoAI

Spatial data processing, machine learning, deep learning, image classification and automated analytical workflows.

Natural hazards and land deformation

Spatial assessment of drought, salinity, flooding, coastal hazards and satellite-derived land motion.

Technical training and knowledge transfer

Practical learning materials, reproducible workflows and potential training collaborations in GIS, remote sensing and Python.

These areas describe potential applications and collaboration opportunities.

Applied peace

Connecting nature, knowledge and humanity

The concept of Applied Peace within CNCAP recognises the relationships between environmental sustainability, human well-being, and peaceful coexistence. Environmental degradation, climate change, natural hazards, and competition over natural resources can affect livelihoods, community resilience and social stability.

Inspired in part by Buddhist approaches to peace and the work of Venerable Thầy Huyền Diệu, the Centre seeks to transform knowledge into practical actions that benefit ecosystems, communities, and future generations.

CNCAP embraces the principles of compassion, non-harm, mutual understanding, cooperation, and responsibility towards the natural environment. These principles inform its approach to scientific research, education and knowledge sharing.

Through applied research and open education, the Centre aims to contribute to environmental stewardship, equitable access to scientific knowledge, sustainable resource management, and peaceful relationships between people and nature.

Environmental peacebuilding, environmental security, and community resilience are emerging areas of interest within CNCAP. They offer opportunities to explore how environmental science and geospatial technologies can contribute to cooperation, sustainable development, and human well-being.

Meet the founder

Dr Thuong Tran

Founder and Researcher

Dr Thuong Tran is a geospatial scientist, researcher, and educator specialising in remote sensing, GIS, Earth observation, spatial modelling, and GeoAI. He holds a PhD in Geospatial Sciences from Monash University, Australia, and has research and university-level teaching experience in Australia and Viet Nam.

His doctoral research examined mangrove dynamics along the southern coast of Viet Nam using spectral remote sensing and geospatial analysis. His broader research interests include coastal ecosystems, vegetation dynamics, agricultural drought, soil salinity, land use and land cover change, spatial statistics, and satellite radar interferometry for monitoring land deformation.

He has approximately a decade of university-level teaching experience in Viet Nam and research experience at Monash University. His technical interests include ArcGIS Pro, Google Earth Engine, Python, spatial statistical modelling, and machine learning. He received the 2025 Esri Australia Young Scholar Award.

Through CNCAP, he aims to share his research and teaching experience, develop accessible geospatial learning resources, and connect scientific methods with practical environmental and industry needs.

CNCAP is currently a single-person initiative. Dr Thuong Tran is responsible for its research direction, educational content, website, and resource development. CNCAP welcomes future collaboration.
